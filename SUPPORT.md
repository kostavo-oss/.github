# Support

**A question, a bug, an idea** — open an issue in the tool's repository. The people who
build it read them, and the answer is there for the next person.

**Help with your own setup** — the tools are made by [Kostavo](https://kostavo.com), and
Kostavo does this as paid work: putting the tools to work with your team, and the
Databricks platform around them. [Get in touch](https://kostavo.com/contact/).

Nothing in the tools depends on that. They are Apache-2.0 and complete as they are.
